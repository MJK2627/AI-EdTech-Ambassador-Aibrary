## About Aibrary

Aibrary turns non-fiction books and expert insights into 10–15 minute audio micro-learning sessions. Available in 160+ countries on iOS and Android.

**Three ways to learn:**
| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Summary** | Quick breakdown of key takeaways |
| **Curated Podcast** | Conversational, easy-to-digest audio discussion |
| **Idea Twin** ⭐ | Standout feature — an AI "cognitive mirror" that debates and deep-dives with you in your own voice, turning passive listening into active engagement |

**AI Growth Team:** Nova (mindset & habits), Orion (knowledge & research), Atlas (execution & goals) — three AI avatars acting as 24/7 virtual mentors.

**Also notable:**
- "For You" personalized daily feed
- My Growth Plan — adaptive learning roadmap by focus area
- Search by Photo — scan a book cover to get its AI podcast instantly
- Gamified streaks and milestones to build consistency

Built on established learning theory — Vygotsky's Zone of Proximal Development and Perkins' Active Learning — rather than plain summarization.
